To balance local Chinese utility apps with global uncensored communication (WhatsApp, Google Scholar, Gmail, YouTube), hardware is strictly separated across four devices:

### 📱 Device 1: iPhone 17 (Primary Daily Lifeline)

*   **Proxy Client:** **Shadowrocket** (Must be purchased and downloaded via a non-Chinese Apple ID in Sri Lanka before departure, pre-loaded with the active **Ji Chang** node subscription).

### 📱 Device 2: Samsung S20 FE (The Sri Lankan OTP Vault)

*   **Network:** Houses the primary Sri Lankan **Dialog SIM** with **SMS Roaming Activated**.

### 💻 Device 3: Dell Latitude 5410 (Primary Windows Workstation)
