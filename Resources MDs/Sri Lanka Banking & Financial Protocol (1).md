## 2. The Dialog SIM & OTP Vault Strategy (`Samsung S20 FE`)

Whenever she links a card or makes a larger payment via Alipay/WeChat in China, BOC, People's Bank, or NSB will send an **SMS One-Time Password (OTP)** to her Sri Lankan phone number.
*   [ ] **1. Activate Dialog International SMS Roaming:** Visit a Dialog outlet or verify via the MyDialog app in Sri Lanka that **International Roaming (specifically for incoming SMS)** is active on her primary Dialog SIM card, and keep a small LKR credit balance topped up so the line stays active.
*   [ ] **2. Insert Dialog SIM into the `Samsung S20 FE`:** Keep the Dialog SIM dedicated inside the Samsung S20 FE.
*   [ ] **3. Permanently Disable Mobile Data Roaming:** Go into the Samsung S20 FE settings and turn **Mobile Data OFF** and **Data Roaming OFF**. Incoming SMS texts are free/minimal on roaming, whereas accidental background data roaming in China will drain her entire LKR balance in minutes.

## 3. Alipay & WeChat Pay Binding (Must Be Completed in Sri Lanka)

Foreign Visa and Mastercard cards are much easier to bind and verify from a clean Sri Lankan connection before entering the Great Firewall.

### Alipay (`支付宝`) Setup Checklist (On `iPhone 17`)
*   [ ] **1. Register with Phone Number:** Sign up on Alipay and switch the interface language to **English** (`Me -> Settings -> General -> Language -> English`).
*   [ ] **2. Complete Passport Identity Verification:** Go to `Account & Security -> Identity Information` and upload a clear scan of her **Sri Lankan Passport** (`PARANA GAMAGE NAVINI RAVISHKA PRIYANTHA`). Confirm the status shows **Verified**.
*   [ ] **3. Bind Authorised Sri Lankan Cards:** Go to `Bank Cards -> Add Card` and add her unlocked **BOC, People's Bank, and/or NSB Visa/Mastercard** cards (add at least two cards so she has an instant backup).
*   [ ] **4. Verify "TourCard" or "DiDi Travel" Mini-Program:** Open the **DiDi Travel** mini-program inside Alipay once and accept the terms so it is ready to use in Haikou without any pop-ups.

### WeChat Pay (`微信支付`) Setup Checklist (On `iPhone 17`)
*   [ ] **1. Enable WeChat Pay / Wallet:** Go to `Me -> Pay and Services -> Wallet`.
*   [ ] **2. Complete Real-Name Passport Verification:** Upload her Sri Lankan Passport details exactly as printed on the passport bio page (`PARANA GAMAGE NAVINI RAVISHKA PRIYANTHA`).
*   [ ] **3. Link Sri Lankan Visa/Mastercard:** Add her primary and backup Sri Lankan bank cards under `Bank Cards` and complete the SMS OTP check using the code received on her **Samsung S20 FE**.
